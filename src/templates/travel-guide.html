01 · When to go

The river changes the experience

The waterfall’s volume varies through the year. High water brings heavy spray that can obscure the full view from the paths. At lower water, more of the cliff face and gorge is visible. There is no single view that every visitor sees.

Choose your dates around your priorities rather than a promise of perfect conditions. Rafting routes are also affected by water levels; flights depend on suitable weather. Leave some flexibility for activities that might need to move.

02 · An itinerary idea

Three nights, without rushing

This is a suggested outline, not a fixed package. Adjust it to flight times, available departures, your accommodation and how active you want to be.

  1. Arrive & settle in

    Leave time for the transfer and check-in. If arrival allows, a sunset cruise can be a first introduction to the upper river; otherwise keep the evening free.

  2. The Falls & an open afternoon

    Make the rainforest walk the main event. Consider a helicopter flight for a second perspective, or spend the afternoon at your hotel. The Boma is an option for the evening.

  3. A wildlife or adventure day

    Choose a game drive with free time afterwards, or devote the day to rafting. A Chobe day trip is another full-day alternative; avoid squeezing it between other timed outings.

  4. Breakfast & onward travel

    Plan around the airport transfer and any border crossing. Keep this morning simple, especially if your onward flight is early.

03 · Location matters

Choose a base for your days

Town-area hotels and garden lodges suit visitors heading out for activities most days. Riverfront hotels offer a quieter setting, but check the shuttle or transfer arrangements. A national-park lodge may include meals and game activities, which changes both the budget and the itinerary.

For families, confirm room configuration and child policies before comparing totals. For access requirements, ask about the actual room, paths, vehicles and boat boarding arrangements.

04 · Around the table

Plan the evening as well as the day

A meal overlooking the gorge, high tea at a hotel or a town restaurant can each suit a different part of the itinerary. Check travel time from your accommodation and reserve if the meal is tied to a fixed activity or performance.

The Boma combines dinner and entertainment. Simunye is a theatre production, so dinner is a separate plan. Use the venue’s current information for menus, serving times and reservations.

05 · Arrival & departure

Leave time for the airport

Airport lounges are terminal services, separate from the town’s restaurants and bars. Before relying on lounge access, check your terminal, opening times and entry eligibility with your airline or lounge provider.

Give your transfer provider the flight details and leave enough time for check-in, security and any onward border crossing. Keep departure-day activities flexible.

06 · Ask before you confirm

Get the full picture

What is included?
Ask for a quotation showing meals, drinks, transfers, activities and mandatory park or conservation charges.
Which programme am I booking?
Confirm the operator, departure point, time allowed, participation requirements and cancellation terms for that specific product.
Does my itinerary cross a border?
For Chobe or activities accessed from another country, confirm the entry and re-entry documents required for your own travel arrangements.
What happens if conditions change?
Check the operator’s weather and river-level policy, and leave space in the itinerary where possible.
