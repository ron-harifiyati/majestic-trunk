01About this notice

Majestic Trunk Expeditions helps visitors explore activities, accommodation and dining in and around Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe. This policy explains the information involved when you browse the website, build a trip shortlist or contact us about your visit.

The website is an enquiry and planning service. An enquiry does not confirm availability, reserve a room or table, or create a confirmed booking.

02What we collect

The contact form asks for:

  • Your name and email address, so we can identify your enquiry and reply. These are required.
  • A phone or WhatsApp number, if you choose to provide one.
  • Proposed arrival and departure dates. These are optional if your dates are undecided.
  • The number of adults and children travelling, to help us understand your group.
  • Your selected experiences, places to stay and dining interests, if you have saved any.
  • An optional message, with your questions or preferences.

You can enquire without selecting anything from the catalogue. If travelling with children, you may include their ages to help with suitable suggestions. Please keep your initial message to the information needed for your enquiry; do not include passport copies, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

The website does not have customer accounts, a payment form or document uploads.

Children and other travellers

The form asks for the number of children in your group and lets you include their ages in your message. It does not have a separate form for children. If you enquire for other travellers, include only the details needed to discuss the trip.

03Enquiries and how we use them

On the current public version, the form prepares an email containing the details you enter and your selected items. You send it using your chosen email application, or copy it into your preferred email service.

Completing the form or preparing the message does not, by itself, send an enquiry to Majestic Trunk. Your email provider processes the message when you choose to send it.

When received, the information helps us understand your interests, respond to questions and discuss possible travel arrangements. The business owner will initially be the only person reading and replying to enquiries.

If direct website email delivery is introduced, this policy will be updated to explain that process and the service involved.

04Saved trips and browser storage

“Your trip” uses your browser’s local storage to remember the catalogue items you select. It stores item identifiers so your shortlist remains available as you browse and return to the site.

The application does not save your name, email, phone number, travel dates or message in local or session storage. Your browser or email application may offer its own autofill or draft-saving features under its settings.

You can remove individual items from your shortlist or clear this website’s stored data through your browser settings. Saved choices remain until removed, cleared or otherwise deleted by the browser.

The site’s own code does not set advertising cookies or include analytics or advertising trackers. This does not mean that hosting or external services process no technical information.

05Services and sharing

Our website and email services process information needed to deliver their services. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs IP addresses for security. Google Fonts supplies the typefaces and receives your IP address when your browser requests them. Your email provider handles the enquiry you choose to send.

Read the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Fonts privacy explanation for more about those services. Linked hotels, activity operators and other external websites have their own privacy practices.

Majestic Trunk’s receiving mailbox provider and the arrangements for sharing enquiry information with travel providers are still being finalised. Hosting, font and email services may process information outside Zimbabwe; the final provider and transfer arrangements will be added when confirmed.

06Replies and marketing

We use your contact details to respond about the trip or services you have asked us about. We do not use enquiry details to send newsletters, promotional offers or unrelated marketing messages.

07How long information is kept

Your saved shortlist remains in your browser as explained above. Enquiries you send are separate email records; clearing your browser shortlist does not delete an email already sent.

The business is still deciding how long received enquiries and any later booking records will be kept. Those periods or retention criteria will be added before this draft is finalised. Hosting and email providers also have their own retention practices.

08Protecting your information

The public website uses HTTPS, and the application keeps contact-form details out of its saved shortlist. These measures do not make online communication completely secure.

Majestic Trunk Expeditions is operated by one person. Within the business, access to enquiry accounts and devices is limited to the owner. The planned enquiry mailbox will use two-factor authentication at launch. Please avoid sending sensitive documents or payment details in your initial enquiry.

09Your requests and concerns

You can ask how your information is used, request access or correction, request deletion, object to processing, or raise a privacy concern. Requests are considered under the applicable law and the information actually held. Some records may need to be retained where a legal requirement applies.

If you have a complaint, contact us using the details below. You can also raise a data-protection concern with POTRAZ, Zimbabwe’s Data Protection Authority.
