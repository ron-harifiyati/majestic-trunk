Activities, accommodation and trip planning for Victoria Falls and the surrounding Zambezi region.

Start with what you want to do

Some visitors come for a day at the Falls. Others want river time, a wildlife drive and a few nights in a lodge. Majestic Trunk brings those choices together so you can work out the shape of your trip before booking.

Our activity and accommodation guides explain the differences between the options, from the physical demands of rafting to the inclusions in a safari lodge package. The practical details are as much a part of the decision as the setting.

How to plan with us

Browse the guides, then send an enquiry with your dates, group size and priorities. For products available to reserve online, bookings are handled through our Activitar catalogue. Specific operators, availability, prices and terms are confirmed for the selected booking.
