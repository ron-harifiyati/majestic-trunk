Majestic Trunk Expeditions is a tour operator, booking partner and trip planner for Victoria Falls and the Zambezi region.

Come to us for a single experience or help bringing several parts of your visit together. We help you explore the options, compare accommodation and fit your chosen outings into the time you have.

For each arrangement, we confirm the provider, what’s included and the booking terms, so you can review the details before deciding.
