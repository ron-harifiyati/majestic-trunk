About us

Your expert on the ground in Victoria Falls

A local team turning the wonder of the Zambezi into effortless, expertly guided journeys.

Majestic Trunk Expeditions is built on a simple idea: the best way to experience Victoria Falls is with the people who live here. We're a small, local team who grew up beside the Zambezi and know its guides, lodges and hidden corners by name.

We don't sell off-the-shelf packages. We listen to how you like to travel, then build a trip around it — matching you with the right experiences, the right lodge and the right pace, and staying on call from arrival to departure.

What we do

From Grade-5 rafting and sunrise game drives to sunset cruises and community village visits, we curate and book the very best of the region — and handle the logistics so you don't have to.
