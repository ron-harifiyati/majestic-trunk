About Majestic Trunk Expeditions

Your Victoria Falls tour operator & trip planner.

We combine activity bookings, accommodation arrangements and personal itinerary planning for visitors to Victoria Falls and the Zambezi region.

What we do for you

Majestic Trunk Expeditions combines the roles of tour operator, booking partner and trip planner. You can book a single experience or ask us to bring several parts of your visit together.

We help you choose activities, compare accommodation and shape an itinerary around your dates, group and interests. For each arrangement, we confirm who provides the experience or stay, what is included and which booking terms apply.

Activities & tours

From a Falls walk to a river cruise or a safari outing, we help organise your chosen experiences. Products available to reserve online are listed in our Activitar catalogue. Other arrangements begin with an enquiry.

Accommodation arrangements

We help you compare hotels and lodges, identify suitable room options and request quotations for your dates. Availability, meal plans, included activities and additional charges are confirmed for the particular stay.

Personal trip planning

Share your ideas, whether you have a full itinerary in mind or are still choosing where to begin. We help fit the activities and accommodation together, leaving time for travel between them.

How to get started

Send your dates, number of travellers, accommodation preferences and the activities that interest you. We can discuss options and a quotation before you confirm your arrangements.
