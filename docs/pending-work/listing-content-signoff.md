## Scope
Obtain final content sign-off for all 78 listings: 26 experiences, 34 accommodation properties and 18 dining venues.

Existing records have source links and reviewed dates. Successful HTTP requests do not verify every factual claim or establish a business partnership.

## Specific gap found
The “Before you book” sections were intentionally removed from experience/accommodation pages. Some participation guidance now exists only in the JSON planning data, including age/weight/health checks for bridge/gorge activities and swimming/fitness guidance for water activities. The experience template no longer renders that planning data.

Do not restore the removed sections wholesale. Decide which essential notes belong beside the relevant facts or enquiry action, and which must be confirmed in the quotation.
