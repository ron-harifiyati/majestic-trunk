## Scope
Create owner-approved terms for the actual services Majestic Trunk offers. Keep these separate from the Privacy Policy. The current planner is an enquiry shortlist, with no checkout, payment or guaranteed reservation.

## Business information needed
- [ ] Legal/trading name and actual role for each service: operator, agent or planner.
- [ ] Who supplies each service and who contracts with the traveller.
- [ ] When a quotation becomes a booking, how written acceptance works and when availability is confirmed.
- [ ] Currency, taxes/fees, deposits, payment process and quotation validity.
- [ ] Cancellation, refund, amendment, no-show and provider-cancellation procedures.
- [ ] Handling of weather, seasonal access, minimum ages, participation restrictions and border requirements.
- [ ] Inclusion/exclusion and transfer responsibilities.
- [ ] Customer-support and complaint contacts and applicable legal review.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Draft terms only from confirmed business/provider arrangements; do not invent refund periods or blanket liability exclusions.
- [ ] Explain that adding items to the trip planner or sending an enquiry does not reserve services or tables.
- [ ] Explain that multiple accommodation choices can be alternatives for comparison.
- [ ] Ensure any provider-specific conditions are disclosed before a customer commits.
- [ ] Have the final terms reviewed for the applicable law and approved by the owner.
- [ ] Publish readable terms with an effective date and appropriate footer/contact/quotation links.
- [ ] Keep terms consistent with enquiry wording and the eventual booking process.
